Diagnosis Lab

Human, AI or both: who finds the real cause of a business event, and at what cost in expert time. Rules v1.1.
Connecting to Claude and the results database…

1. AI attempts

Runs the AI-alone diagnosis three times per case. Attempt 1 is the AI-alone result; all three feed the router. Answers stay hidden here so they cannot influence your own runs. You can start this and keep working: it runs in the background while this page stays open.

2. Dry run on practice cases

Try both modes on these three cases. You may see everything here; nothing on these cases is scored as evidence.

3. Test session

20 cases in random order: 10 you diagnose alone, 10 as a pair with the AI. Each has 3 minutes; after that the clock turns red and you get 1 more minute for a last change (overtime is recorded). A short break is offered every 7 cases.

Diagnose a new event

Describe what happened, not why. The router decides whether the AI answers alone, asks you to review its candidates, or hands the case to you.

Score the runs

Only press this after every AI attempt and every test case is finished. The scorer sees the answer key and the answers, never which setup produced them.
